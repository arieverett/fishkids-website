Kid Tested, Research Approved

Combining medical research in child psychology along with top-notch engineering solutions and design heuristics, we created the best possible games that would develop all aspects of your kid’s fine motor skills. There are 3 main aspects to fine motor skills: 1. hand-eye coordination, 2. manipulating objects, and 3. thumb/fore-finger pressure. Each of our games prioritizes 1 of these aspects, while addressing the other 2. When combined, it creates the strongest possible development across the spectrum of fine motor skills.
